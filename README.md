## 🚀 What I'm Building

### 🚚 PEPE: Logistics Software for Kenya & East/Central Africa
I worked with a team to create **PEPE**, a logistics platform that aims to make moving cargo across Kenya and East & Central Africa seamless for institutions, drivers and every stakeholder in the chain.

**The idea in short:**
- **Execution:** routing, tracking and ETAs
- **Trust:** verification, ratings and performance history for shippers and transporters
- **Records:** digital proof of delivery, invoices and logs
- **Marketplace:** smart matching of loads to transporters
- **Payments:** an escrow-style flow with M-Pesa and bank integration, so the platform oversees the release of funds between shipper and transporter
- **Access:** simple SMS and WhatsApp-friendly experience, with a corridor-first rollout
